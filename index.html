A little story about why we began

We know what it feels like to start over.

We started TableMates because we understand how lonely Japan can feel when the language is unfamiliar and home is far away. Even simple things can feel harder when you don't yet know the words or the people around you.

We believe friendship can begin around a shared meal, a cup of tea, or a new experience. Our gatherings bring people from Japan and around the world together to enjoy good food, share stories, and make friends—so little by little, a faraway place can start to feel like home.

Kyoto · Gion

Tea travels. Gathered at one table.

Our tea ceremony menu brings favorite brews from Japan and around the world to Kyoto.

Choose a pot to share or try a tasting flight. Your host will brew each tea with care and introduce where it comes from. The selection of seasonal sweets is included.

Japan · Kyoto & Shizuoka

  • Uji matchaStone-ground green tea, whisked to a smooth, bright finish.
  • Uji gyokuroShade-grown leaves with a sweet, mellow umami.
  • Shizuoka senchaFresh-steamed everyday green tea with gentle grassy notes.
  • HojichaRoasted green tea with a warm, toasty aroma.
  • GenmaichaGreen tea blended with toasted rice for a soft, nutty cup.

Bangladesh

  • Sylhet black teaFull-bodied tea grown among the gardens of northeastern Bangladesh.
  • Bangladeshi milk chaStrong brewed tea simmered with milk and warming spices.

Nepal

  • Ilam orthodox black teaFragrant high-grown leaves from the eastern Himalayan foothills.
  • Nepali chiyaComforting milk tea simmered with cardamom, ginger, and cloves.

Sri Lanka · Ceylon

  • Uva high-grown teaBright, brisk black tea from Sri Lanka’s eastern highlands.
  • Nuwara Eliya teaLight, floral highland tea with a delicate finish.

India

  • Assam second flushRich, malty black tea from India’s Brahmaputra Valley.
  • Darjeeling first flushDelicate spring tea with light floral and muscatel notes.
  • Masala chaiRobust black tea infused with ginger, cinnamon, and cardamom.

China

  • Jasmine pearlsGreen tea scented with fragrant jasmine blossoms.
  • Tieguanyin oolongFloral, gently roasted oolong from Fujian.

Tea and wagashi selections can change with the season and local availability. Please let your host know about allergies when booking.
